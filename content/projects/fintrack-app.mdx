FinTrack makes personal finance less intimidating. The app auto-categorizes expenses, visualizes spending trends, and helps users set realistic budgets.

Built with Flutter for a native feel on both platforms. Firebase provides real-time sync and secure authentication.

The chart system was custom-built to feel approachable — rounded edges, warm colors, and animations that make checking your finances almost enjoyable.
