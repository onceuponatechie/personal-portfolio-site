Vintage Store is a boutique e-commerce experience for curated second-hand fashion. Each collection is styled as a lookbook with editorial photography.

Built on Shopify with custom Liquid templates, the store prioritizes visual storytelling over traditional product grids.

The design draws from vintage magazine layouts — editorial typography, film-grain textures, and a muted color palette.
