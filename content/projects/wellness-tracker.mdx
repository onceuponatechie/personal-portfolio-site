Wellness Tracker is a personal project that became a daily companion. It combines habit tracking, mood logging, and gratitude journaling into one calming interface.

The app was designed with mindfulness in mind — soft colors, gentle animations, and no notification overload. Built with React Native for cross-platform reach.

Firebase handles sync and authentication, making it effortless for users to pick up where they left off on any device.
