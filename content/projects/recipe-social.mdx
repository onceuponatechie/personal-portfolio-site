Recipe Social reimagines recipe sharing as a social experience. Users create visual recipe cards, follow their favorite home cooks, and build personalized cookbooks.

The platform prioritizes beautiful food photography and clean ingredient lists. Search is powered by tags and dietary filters.

Built with a React frontend and Node.js API, with PostgreSQL for relational data like followers, likes, and collections.
