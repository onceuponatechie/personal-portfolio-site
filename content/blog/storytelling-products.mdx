Every product is a story. The landing page is Act 1. The onboarding is Act 2. The moment the user gets value — that's the climax.

Most products fail at storytelling because they focus on features instead of feelings. Nobody cares that your app has 'real-time sync.' They care that their work is always up to date, no matter which device they grab.

Here's my framework: Start with the struggle. What's the user's life like before your product? Paint that picture vividly. Then introduce your product as the guide — not the hero. The user is always the hero.

Finally, show the transformation. Before and after. Pain and relief. Chaos and clarity.

The best products don't need to explain what they do. They show you what you become.
