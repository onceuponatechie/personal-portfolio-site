You don't need a team of 10 designers to benefit from a design system. In fact, solo creators might need one even more.

When you're doing everything yourself — design, development, content — consistency is the first thing to slip. A design system is your safety net.

Start small: Pick your colors (max 5), choose your fonts (max 2), define your spacing scale (4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64). That's it. That's a design system.

Next level: Create component variants. Your button doesn't need 47 states. It needs default, hover, disabled, and maybe a secondary variant.

Document it. I use a single Notion page with color swatches, font samples, and component screenshots. Takes 30 minutes to set up, saves hours every week.

The goal isn't perfection — it's reducing decisions. Every decision you don't have to make is energy saved for the work that matters.
