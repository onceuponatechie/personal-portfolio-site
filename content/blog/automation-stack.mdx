I'm lazy. Productively lazy. Here's every automation that saves me time each week:

1. Email to Notion: Every newsletter I subscribe to gets auto-saved to a Notion database with AI-generated summaries.

2. Social scheduling: I batch-create content on Sundays, and Buffer handles the rest of the week.

3. Invoice generation: Stripe webhook → Zapier → Google Docs template → email to client. Zero manual work.

4. Meeting prep: Calendar event triggers a Notion page with attendee info pulled from LinkedIn.

5. Weekly review: Every Friday, a Make scenario compiles my GitHub commits, tweets, and Notion tasks into a single summary.

Total time saved per week: roughly 8 hours. That's a whole extra workday I get back just by setting things up once.
