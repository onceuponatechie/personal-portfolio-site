It hit me on a Tuesday. I opened Figma, stared at the canvas, and felt... nothing. No excitement, no ideas, no motivation. Just a blinking cursor and a deadline.

Creative burnout isn't about being tired. It's about being empty. You've given everything to the work and forgotten to refill the well.

Here's what helped me recover:

1. I stopped consuming design content for two weeks. No Dribbble, no Twitter threads, no 'Top 10 UI Trends' articles.

2. I went for walks without my phone. Boredom is the birthplace of creativity.

3. I made something with no purpose. A collage. A doodle. A playlist. Things with no client, no deadline, no audience.

4. I talked to a friend about it. Turns out, everyone goes through this. Knowing that helped more than any productivity hack.

Burnout is your brain telling you to slow down. Listen to it.
