Day 1: I tweeted 'I'm going to build in public for the next 30 days.' Zero likes. Zero replies. Just me and the void.

Day 7: I shared a screenshot of something broken. It got more engagement than anything I'd ever posted. People love seeing the messy middle.

Day 15: Someone I admired reached out to collaborate. Building in public isn't just about accountability — it's about visibility.

Day 21: I wanted to quit. The pressure of daily updates was real. But I realized nobody was actually counting except me.

Day 30: I had shipped 4 micro-projects, gained 200 followers, and made 2 new friends. More importantly, I'd built a habit of shipping.

The lesson? Consistency beats perfection. Show your work, even when it's embarrassing. Especially when it's embarrassing.
